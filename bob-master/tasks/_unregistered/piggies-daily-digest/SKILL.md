## Steps
1. Maintain digest_state.json in the folder (analyzed transcript filenames + "known_spam" list + "ads_account_id"). Analyze only new files.
2. SPAM SCREENING FIRST (per SOP): read EVERY new call transcript including voicemails on unanswered calls — robocalls leave voicemails too. Spam/telemarketers = NOT A LEAD: exclude from callbacks, missed-call counts, answer-rate math, CPL/CPQL, and projections; list under exclusions with the reason. A voicemail with a missing transcript is UNREVIEWED, not a lead. Grow the known_spam list.
3. Classify remaining calls from CONTENT, not duration: QUALIFIED LEAD (real HVAC project/service discussion — repair, replacement, install, maintenance — buying intent or appointment set; extract caller name, city, job type, urgency, budget signals, appointment); MISSED/VOICEMAIL-LOST (never reached a human, including "completed" calls whose transcript is only the business voicemail greeting — learn Piggies' greeting text from the first such transcripts and note it in digest_state.json under "voicemail_greeting"); NOT A LEAD (wrong numbers, vendors). Deduplicate by phone number. Reference recording files (recordings/<filename>) for notable calls.
4. Track open deals: appointment commitments in transcripts → verify follow-through via later calls and GHL opportunity stage when reachable; flag stalled deals with a specific next action. No known open deals yet — build this list as transcripts reveal them.
5. Google Ads attribution (when the ads account is available): match yesterday's ad calls to transcript/GHL calls — timestamps within 3 minutes AND (durations within 15s OR both voicemail/no-answer/missed), area codes equal when both present, unambiguous 1:1 only. Tag matched GHL contacts (if reachable): ads-attributed, camp:/adgrp: short lowercase tags, no duplicates. Never fabricate matches.
6. MANDATORY Answer-Rate Angle (per SOP): % of real calls (spam excluded) hitting voicemail → % of answered callers who qualified → "likely a large share of missed callers were qualified buyers too" (framed as likely, never fact) → tracked CPQL vs projected CPQL (modeled as ~half of missed unique real callers qualifying, always labeled "modeled, not tracked — unanswered calls leave nothing to verify") → the Google smart-bidding argument (long connected calls are conversion signals that teach Google who the buyer is; unanswered calls train it toward the wrong audience — every voicemail is training data steering the campaign away from buyers it already found).
7. Digest sections, most actionable first: URGENT CALLBACKS (last 72h, spam screened out, with phone numbers) / OPEN DEALS / NEW QUALIFIED LEADS (who, what, key quotes, attribution, recording reference) / THE ANSWER-RATE ANGLE with current numbers / AD ATTRIBUTION (one auditable timestamp/duration pair, or "no ad calls yesterday") / SPAM & NOT-A-LEAD exclusions (one line each). If nothing new at all, say so in two lines.
